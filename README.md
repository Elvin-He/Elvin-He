---

### 🔥 Current Research

#### 1. SCOPE-Q — Reliable Quantum Calibration
> *Decision-making and calibration under partial observability in quantum systems.*

SCOPE-Q studies how a calibration system should decide **what to probe, what to trust, when to tune, and when to abstain** when the underlying hardware state is only partially observable.

**Research themes:**  
`Quantum Calibration` · `Bayesian Decision Making` · `Adaptive Probing` · `Safe Control` · `Quantum Systems`

---

#### 2. [MagNA](https://github.com/Elvin-He/magna-compiler) — Fault-Tolerant Quantum Compilation
> *Heterogeneous logical-gate compilation for magic-state distillation on neutral-atom qLDPC codes.*

MagNA explores compiler support for combining heterogeneous fault-tolerant logical primitives — including **code automorphisms, transversal operations, measurements, and magic-state injection** — on neutral-atom architectures.

**Research themes:**  
`qLDPC Codes` · `Quantum Compiler` · `Neutral Atoms` · `Logical Gates` · `Magic-State Distillation`

---

#### 3. Dyndim — Dynamic QEC Compilation
> *Compiler and architecture support for dynamically switching quantum error-correcting code structures.*

Dyndim explores whether a fault-tolerant system can dynamically move between code structures with different dimensionality or connectivity to expose useful logical operations while respecting physical hardware constraints.

**Research themes:**  
`Quantum Error Correction` · `Code Switching` · `Fault-Tolerant Computing` · `Compiler/Architecture Co-design`

---

#### 4. [QControlLLM](https://github.com/Rodin1000/QcontrolLLM) — AI for Quantum Control
> *Large language models for reasoning and optimization in quantum-control tasks.*

I contributed to the development of LLM-based quantum-control methods and evaluation infrastructure, including numerical representations for high-fidelity control regimes and simulation-based evaluation pipelines.

**Research themes:**  
`Quantum Control` · `AI for Science` · `LLM Agents` · `QuTiP`
